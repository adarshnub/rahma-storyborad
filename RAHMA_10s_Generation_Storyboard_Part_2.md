## Pre-generation visual lock

This section is not allowed to introduce a new visual language. It must continue directly from Part 1.

- **Format:** Every accepted generation frame is landscape 16:9. Reject portrait or square outputs, however strong the image is. A wider generated still may be conformed non-destructively to 16:9 only when the required action, character and location continuity all remain intact after crop and the cropped result passes visual QA.
- **Cast:** Every on-screen woman, man and child is Emirati, with an authentic contemporary UAE appearance. Reuse the established Part 1 cast wherever a role fits. Create a new cast member only when the scenario genuinely requires one, and establish that character on an approved Emirati continuity board before using them in frames.
- **Camera:** Intimate, human and close. Never a surveillance view, overhead tracking shot, distant wide, or screen-only shot.
- **Tone:** Quiet attention already in place. Never a rescue spectacle.
- **Hard exclusions:** No sirens, flashing lights, crowd/search-party scenes, rushing, running, panic, falls, gasps, medical gore, vehicle spectacle, logos, readable incidental text, or generated on-screen typography.
- **Palette and material:** Muted indigo shadows, cream limestone, walnut, restrained teal, amber practicals; gentle 35mm grain, natural skin texture.
- **Rahma device:** Use `REF-04-device-and-wearable-v2.png` as the exact device authority. Graphite-black phone; deep-indigo UI; one large amber-gold round hold button; only a soft teal confirmation pulse. Screens are secondary, sparse and non-readable. Any title-card wording, exact RAHMA lettering, map markers, or medical information is compositor-supplied in post, never generated by image AI.
- **Reference locations:** Continue the domestic and service-world materials from `REF-02-home-environments.png` and `REF-03-service-locations.png`. New locations must look like adjacent places in the same UAE/GCC world, not a new commercial, cityscape, or generic stock environment.

## Three distinct age groups

The source draft had a child and two senior characters. To meet the current direction, the three use cases are now explicitly different age groups:

| Moment | Lead age group | Lead character | What Rahma proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One - The Child | Teenager, 13–14 | Emirati teenage boy | A teenager triggers Rahma from a safe building corridor during a fire response; fire-rescue and his parents reach him. |
| Two - The Medicine | Working-age adult | Lina (established Part 1 cast) | Prevention: a routine medicine refill arrives before a shortage becomes a problem. |
| Three - The Night | Senior citizen, 81 | Ahmed | A calm, restrained medical response begins before his daughter has to explain anything. |

Mariam remains the senior continuity character introduced in Part 1; she does not become the lead of Moment Two. Lina takes the working-age role in Moment Two, avoiding both age-group duplication and an unnecessary new cast member.

## Generation acceptance checklist

Before any frame enters `reference-images/`, verify every item:

1. It is a landscape 16:9 still.
2. Face, age, wardrobe and location exactly match the relevant approved continuity board.
3. Phone, wearable and UI exactly match REF-04; all exact graphic information is supplied in compositing.
4. The shot is close and human, not detached or surveillance-like.
5. No prohibited emergency, crowd, or panic imagery appears.
6. The frame advances the specified second's action without inventing another story beat.
